Section 3 — Event Dates & Operating Hours
A single TEN may cover a continuous period of up to 168 hours (7 days), including setup and close-down periods where licensable activities continue. This is relevant for late-night finishes, overnight access, multi-day events, or consecutive programming.
Must include all staff, organisers, stewards and performers. Statutory maximum is 499.
Late notices may be rejected if objected to by the police or environmental health authority.
Section 2 — Nature of Event
Describe the event clearly and specifically. This assists the police and environmental health authority in assessing the notice — e.g. "wedding reception with pay bar and string quartet", "DJ event with recorded music and bar".
This description may exceed the available space on the official PDF form. Consider shortening it for a cleaner export.
