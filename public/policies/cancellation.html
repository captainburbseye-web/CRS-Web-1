Cancellation

Booking Changes

If you need to move or cancel a booking, use the booking route provided at checkout where available, or contact CRS as soon as possible. The more notice given, the more likely a session can be reallocated cleanly.

Service-Specific Terms

Some services may carry their own cancellation or rescheduling terms inside the live booking flow, written quote, or invoice. Where that happens, the service-specific terms take priority.

Operational Changes

If CRS needs to alter or reschedule a booking because of access, maintenance, staffing, or wider build-phase constraints, the team will use the details supplied at booking to arrange the next step.

Need Help?

Use the contact page or email info@crsoxford.com with your booking details and the requested change.
