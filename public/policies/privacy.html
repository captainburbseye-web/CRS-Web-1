Privacy

Cowley Road Studios · 118 Cowley Road, Oxford OX4 1JE, United Kingdom

What We Collect

If you contact CRS through the website, the information you submit may include your name, email address, phone number, enquiry type, and the message you choose to send.

How It Is Used

Submitted details are used to respond to enquiries, route bookings, manage scheduling, and handle studio, venue, or technical requests. Information is not requested unless it supports that process.

Processing

Website enquiry messages are transmitted through the site contact system to the CRS inbox so the team can respond. Booking flows may also pass through third-party checkout or scheduling tools where applicable.

Your Choice

If you want your details updated or deleted from recent enquiry records, contact info@crsoxford.com and include enough detail for the team to identify the request.
