1. Strategic Rationale

The current digital signage acts primarily as a teaser for a future state—specifically, a fixed 2026 opening date for the Workshop Café. Meanwhile, the live website demonstrates a robust, currently operating studio ecosystem across two Oxford locations.

To align with business reality, the public screen must transition from "future promise" to "live infrastructure." Crucially, the Workshop Café is now open for bookings and will be opening regular hours very soon. Therefore, it must be presented as an active, available venue rather than a distant project. The new signage strategy emphasizes immediate capabilities: professional recording, rehearsals, AV support, and venue hire.

2. What Wins Over the Current Signage

  • Faster Comprehension: Delivers a 2-second read from across the room using bolder, tighter copy.
  • Live Capability Over Future Promise: Immediately surfaces what artists and clients can book today.
  • Clarified Footprint: Establishes the dual-location reality (Cowley Road and Cricket Road).
  • Ecosystem Expansion: Properly introduces ODRO Engineering alongside the studio offering.
  • Active Venue Positioning: Highlights the Workshop Café as a live booking opportunity rather than an unlaunched concept.
  • Actionable CTA: Drives foot traffic to actual web bookings rather than leaving passersby at a dead end.

8. Tone & Design Direction

  • Typography: Massive, highly legible sans-serif for headlines (e.g., Space Grotesk or Monument Extended) paired with a clean geometric sans for sub-lines. Avoid thin weights.
  • Hierarchy: The headline must dominate. Sub-lines provide context but should not compete. Keep negative space purposeful, not vacant.
  • Legibility: Maximize contrast. Use muted gold (`#D4AF37`) or stark off-white against deep charcoal (`#111412`). Avoid dark green text on dark charcoal backgrounds.
  • Motion: Keep it utilitarian. Hard cuts or rapid fade transitions between slides. A smooth, relentless horizontal scroll for the ticker. No bouncy UI animations.
  • Decorations: Strip out tiny UI dots or "equalizer" bars unless they convey actual operating status. The screen should feel like a serious broadcast monitor.

9. Rollout Priorities

  1. MVP Data Structure: Establish the independent content repository (the JSON/TS file) separate from the display layer.
  2. Layout Framework: Build the static `ScreenLayout` ensuring the ticker and main slide area fit perfectly within the physical screen's resolution without scaling issues.
  3. Content Population: Inject the new 8-slide array and bottom ticker. Verify legibility at 3-5 meters.
  4. CTA Implementation: Generate and style the QR code slide to ensure it scans reliably under screen glare.
  5. Polish & Deploy: Apply the final CRS color palette, ensure typography scales properly, and deploy to the dedicated Pages/Vercel URL.
